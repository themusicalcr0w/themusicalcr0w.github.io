Coherent households

Household planning and residence allocation are separate. Failed room assignments can roll back, while integrity diagnostics detect duplicate homes and bedrooms.

Memory and dialogue

Characters store observation snapshots. Dialogue context brings together relationships, location, time, personality, and remembered changes instead of treating every character as omniscient.

Consistent snapshots

A 3.5 ms per-frame budget spreads snapshot capture over multiple frames. Mutation hooks preserve point-in-time state before a worker thread handles serialization and disk writing.

Inheritance and lineage

Structured character data records ancestry, inherited traits, and their origins, connecting procedural generation to family relationships.

Live pages, custom rendering

The journal captures live page controls as textures and deforms a mesh during a turn. Button controls and pointer-drag previews support completion, cancellation, and restoration.
